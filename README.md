ServiceHub — Local Service Booking Platform

ServiceHub is a Flutter-based local service booking platform that connects customers with service professionals for home repairs, maintenance, and other on-demand services.

📱 About the Project

ServiceHub simplifies the process of finding, booking, and managing local home services from a single mobile application.

Users can discover services, view service details, select a preferred date and time, manage their addresses, book professionals, track bookings, and review completed services.

The project is built from scratch as an independent portfolio application with a focus on real-world product workflows and scalable Flutter development.

🎯 Problem Statement

Finding a reliable professional for home services can involve searching through multiple sources, comparing options, coordinating schedules, and tracking service requests.

ServiceHub aims to bring these activities into one structured platform.
